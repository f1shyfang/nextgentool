Application Process

01

Stage 1

30min Interview

After reviewing your application, we may invite you for a 30 min call to get to know you as a person. No prep is needed, we just want to meet our authentic self and get to know each other. Are you a fit for us? Are we a fit for you?

No prep is needed and no “technical” questions are asked. It’s just an opportunity for you to tell us why you’d smash the role and to show us your natural curiosity and ambition.

02

Stage 2

Small Task

This is the more technical part. We’ll provide you with a small task to let you tangibly showcase your skills. We value what you can bring to the table with your merit rather than determining your capabilities based on past experience, so this is your opportunity to show us what you can do.

03

Stage 3

45min Interview

The last stage is assessing your technical ability (from a first principles perspective) over a 45 min call, talking over the case study and some additional questions.

04

Stage 4

Take the Next Step!

We’ll send you an email letting you know the results
